## 1. Executive summary

Eight canonical Knowledge Asset records are admitted, each a committed standards or method document verifiable at its cited repository path: the capability documentation standard, the catalog repository pattern standard, the ECF conformance gate definition, the enrichment closure report, the general entity governance standard, the entity-system foundation paper, the metamodel semantics rules, and the release readiness standard. Knowledge forms map onto the metamodel enum honestly (guideline for normative how-to documents; case-study for the closure report).

## 2. Seed selection (FOUNDATIONS D-5)

Every seed is a committed document corroborated at admission, applied across repositories (the citing CRs are named in each description). No tacit know-how is recorded without a verifiable artifact.
